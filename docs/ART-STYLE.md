# Art direction

The apartment pairs oak and walnut with warm plaster, sage seating, terracotta pots, and a mustard heat pad. Rounded cushions and low furniture keep silhouettes readable. The splash uses the live living room scene and introduces Bobo's coat before the first run.

Oak and walnut have separate plank patterns with board joints and fine grain. The atlas also holds woven fabric, matte plaster, glazed bathroom tile, stone, mirrors, pottery, and two abstract prints. The city uses one merged mesh with a 256 px atlas for buildings, foliage, and streets. Cat coats share one generated fur texture. Models, artwork, and textures are made in code.

KART DASH uses an original canvas racetrack, kart, and lap display. It refreshes at an 11 Hz target when in view with a clear sightline. Low quality reduces antialiasing, texture resolution, pixel density, material complexity, and mesh segments.

Scoring adds a small feedback label, chime, and meter update. Reduced-motion preferences remove most UI transitions.
