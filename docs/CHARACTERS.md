# Characters

| Character | Appearance | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bobo | About 15 lb, with black fur, a white bib and paws, a small off-center white forehead dash, and green-gold eyes. | Turns toward the player. Sits, loafs, or rolls, and breathes and blinks. |
| Decoy | About 22 lb with broader shoulders, a black neck and forehead, white feet, and amber eyes. | Uses his own spawn bag. A shout costs one point and adds five leaderboard seconds. |
| Patriot Bobo | Bobo in a small red MAGA cap drawn in code. | Rare three-point encounter. |
| Owner | Stylized adult scaled to 1.91 m and a roughly 200 lb build. Backwards cap, stubble, a subtle mustache, and a plain controller. | Appears seated in one merged mesh. The lap encounter occurs in roughly one in five runs, after the first find. |

The coats use a generated fur texture and sculpted, painted patches. The eyes animate separately from the face. Check the bib and dash at floor level and across a room when revising poses.
